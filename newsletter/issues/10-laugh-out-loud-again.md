Hi {{first_name|there}},

A friend says something funny, and you hold back the laugh. This one is for you.

In a US survey from 2015 to 2018, 61.8% of women said they had leaked in the past 12 months [1].

Last time, you learned the Knack for a sneeze: one push, one squeeze. A laugh is a string of pushes, and a long one can last 10 seconds or more.

So a laugh asks 2 things of your pelvic floor. **Speed**, to close at the first burst. **Stamina**, to stay closed until the last one. When it tires halfway, the leak comes late in the laugh.

What helps:

1. **Squeeze as the laugh starts.** Lift at the first burst, like the Knack. In a small study, a squeeze before a medium cough cut leakage by 98% [2].
2. **Keep a gentle hold.** Keep a light lift until the laugh ends, in place of a full squeeze for 10 seconds.
3. **Build stamina.** Slow holds of 5 to 10 seconds train the hold you need. Quick flicks train the start. Guidelines list at least 3 months of pelvic floor training as the first step for stress leaks [3].

**Try it today: the 10-second laugh hold**

Sit tall. Lift your pelvic floor as if you hold in gas. Keep a light hold while you count out loud, "ha, ha, ha", for 10 seconds. Let go fully. Rest 10 seconds. Do it 3 times.

**A question I hear in clinic**

"What is giggle incontinence?"

It is a rare condition. The whole bladder empties during a strong laugh, and it mostly starts in the teens [4]. It is different from a few drops with a laugh. It needs a doctor, not exercises alone.

**See a doctor if** your whole bladder empties when you laugh, or you leak with no laugh, cough or effort.
