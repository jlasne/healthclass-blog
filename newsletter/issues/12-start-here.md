**Try it today: find your hammock in 60 seconds**

Lie on your back, knees bent. Breathe in for 4 seconds and let your pelvic floor soften. Breathe out for 6 seconds and gently lift, as if holding in gas. Let it go on the next in-breath. Do this 5 times at half effort.

**A question I hear in clinic**

"Am I too old to start?"

Leaks do rise with age. In a national survey, 9.7% of women aged 20 to 39 had a pelvic floor disorder, against 49.7% at 80 and older [3]. Age adds load to the muscles. Training adds strength back. The best year to start is the year you notice it.
