You know the lift and the let-go now. The hard part is counting while you do both.

So there is now a free Kegel timer on our site. It runs in your browser:

1. Pick a routine. New to Kegels? Start with Starter holds.
2. Press Start. While the circle fills, squeeze and lift. While it empties, let go.
3. Breathe out as you lift. Breathe in as you relax.

Turn on Sound for a soft beep each time the phase changes. There is no email to give, and nothing is saved.

Here is the routine I teach. Start with 3 seconds on and 3 seconds off. Build to 5, then 10 seconds, once the shorter hold feels easy. Then add quick flicks: squeeze as hard as you can for 1 second, rest for 1 second. Do 10 holds and 10 flicks, twice a day, while you brush your teeth.

The NICE guideline asks for at least 8 squeezes, 3 times a day, kept up for at least 3 months [1]. In trials, women who trained for 3 to 6 months reported fewer leaks than women who did not [2]. The timer helps with the hard part: doing it every day.

**Try it today: 10 starter holds**

Lie on your back, knees bent. Lift for 3 seconds, as if you hold in gas. Let go for 3 seconds. Do 10. Keep your buttocks and inner thighs relaxed, and breathe through each squeeze.

**A question I hear in clinic**

"Can I do Kegels lying down?"

Yes. Lying down is the easiest place to start. Then do them sitting, then standing, so the muscle works where leaks happen.

**See a doctor if** you feel pain in your pelvis, during a squeeze or after, or pain with sex.
