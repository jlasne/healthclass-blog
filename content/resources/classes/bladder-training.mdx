## Who it is for

Bladder training is for women whose leaks come with a sudden urge, alone or with leaks on effort. In the app, it opens in week 1 of the Bladder leaks class for urge or mixed leaks, and comes back in week 4.

The NICE guideline offers bladder training for at least 6 weeks as the first step for urge or mixed leaks.<Ref n={1} /> In a 1991 trial of 123 women aged 55 and older, it cut leaks by 57% on average.<Ref n={2} />

## The 6 tools

1. **Track your urges.** Log each strong urge: the time, what you were doing, its strength from 1 to 5, and if you leaked.
2. **When an urge comes.** Stop and stay still. Distract yourself, and do 5 quick squeezes if it gets strong. Walk to the toilet once it eases.
3. **Coming home.** Do 3 tasks before the toilet: keys away, shoes off, bag on the hook. Then walk calmly.
4. **Name your bladder.** When it calls too early, tell it: "Not yet. I decide when."
5. **Count your stream.** Count the seconds when you pee. Aim for more than 10, time after time. A typical stream lasts about 20.
6. **Go when you need to.** Pee when your bladder is full, not just in case. Before bed, after sex and before a long trip are fine.

## Before you start

Tell your doctor you are starting bladder training. Urge leaks can have other causes, and your doctor can check if you need any tests first.

To find your starting point, keep a [3-day bladder diary](/resources/tools/bladder-diary). To set times for your trips, use the [bladder training schedule](/resources/tools/bladder-training-schedule).
