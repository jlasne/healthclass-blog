## Who it is for

The class is for women who want to know their pelvic floor and train it from the start. You sit at a desk all day. Your core feels weak after kids. Or you want a strong base before sport.

If leaks are your main worry, the [Bladder leaks class](/resources/classes/bladder-leaks) fits better.

## Why it starts with a check

In a 1991 study, 47 women got a short spoken lesson on Kegels. Then 49% did a clean squeeze. 25% used a squeeze that could make leaks worse.<Ref n={1} />

So week 1 is a self-check, one a day: look, feel, squeeze, let go, and note what you find. Training starts from what you learn about your own body.

## Why this order

A tight muscle has to let go before it can lift with full strength. So you stretch in week 2 and breathe in week 3, before the squeeze in week 4.

Pelvic floor training is the first step guidelines list for stress and mixed leaks, for at least 3 months.<Ref n={2} /> In a Cochrane review, women who trained reported fewer leaks than women who did not.<Ref n={3} /> This app was not part of these studies. Results vary.

## What a day looks like

You open the app. You see your day: one exercise, 5 to 10 minutes. You follow the cues on the screen, then answer a quick check-in.

On the first day of each week, a short lesson comes first. In week 6, you build your plan for life from your own notes.
