## Who it is for

The class is for women who leak when they laugh, sneeze, cough, jump or lift. It also fits mixed leaks, when urges come too.

Not sure which kind you have? The [leak type quiz](/resources/tools/leak-type-quiz) takes 1 minute.

## Why a 6-week class

Pelvic floor training is the first step guidelines list for stress leaks, for at least 3 months.<Ref n={2} /> The 6 weeks build the habit. The routine after week 6 keeps it going.

In a Cochrane review, 56% of women with stress leaks reported cure after 3 to 6 months of training. Without training, 6% did.<Ref n={1} /> This app was not part of these studies. Results vary.

## What a day looks like

You open the app. You see your day: one exercise, 5 to 10 minutes. You follow the cues on the screen, like "Breathe out, then lift." Then you answer 2 quick check-in questions.

On the first day of each week, a short lesson comes first. Week 3 teaches the Knack, a squeeze timed before a cough. In a small study, it cut leakage by 98% on a medium cough.<Ref n={3} />
