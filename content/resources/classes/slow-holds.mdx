## What a slow hold does

A slow hold is a Kegel you lift and keep. It trains stamina: the steady support your pelvic floor needs through a long laugh, a walk or a run.

Quick flicks are its partner. They train speed for a cough or a sneeze. A good routine has both.

## How to do one

1. **Find your position.** Lie on your back, knees bent. It is the easiest place to start.
2. **Breathe out, then lift.** Squeeze as if you hold in gas, and draw the muscle up and in.
3. **Keep the lift** for 3 seconds. Breathe normally while you hold.
4. **Let go fully** for 3 seconds. Feel the muscle soften and drop.
5. **Repeat 10 times.**

## How to build up

- **Seconds:** add 1 second when your hold stays clean to the end. Aim for 10.
- **Positions:** lying down, then sitting, then standing. Standing is where most leaks happen.
- **Days:** the NICE guideline asks for at least 8 squeezes, 3 times a day, for at least 3 months.<Ref n={1} />

## Common mistakes

- **Squeezing your butt or thighs.** Only the pelvic floor works.
- **Holding your breath.** It pushes down. Breathe through the hold.
- **Skipping the rest.** The let-go is half the exercise.
