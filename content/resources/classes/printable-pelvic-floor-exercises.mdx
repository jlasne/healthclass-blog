## What is on the sheet

The routine comes from the Health Class app. It is the short routine you keep after the Bladder leaks class, at its first-week pace.

- **Slow breaths** to soften your belly and your pelvic floor before you start.
- **8 slow holds** for stamina: a long laugh, a walk, a run.
- **10 quick flicks** for speed: a cough or a sneeze.
- **5 Knack coughs** to time a squeeze just before the push. In a 1998 study, this squeeze cut leakage by 98% on a medium cough.<Ref n={2} />
- **A minute to let go.** A muscle has to lengthen before it can lift with full strength.

## How to use it

The NICE guideline asks for at least 8 squeezes, 3 times a day, for at least 3 months.<Ref n={1} /> So the sheet has 1 full routine a day and 2 short sets of 8 holds.

Tie each set to something you already do: your morning coffee, lunch, brushing your teeth. Tick the day on the chart. On a hard day, 1 set of 8 still counts.

## More free sheets

The National Association For Continence (NAFC) is a US non-profit for bladder and bowel health. It offers free downloadable sheets, one exercise each, sorted from easy to difficult, plus a free toolkit with a step-by-step Kegel guide.<Ref n={3} />

NAFC asks you to check with your doctor or physical therapist before you start a new routine. [See the NAFC sheets](https://nafc.org/downloadable-pelvic-floor-exercises/).
