## How to use the timer

1. Pick a routine. New to Kegels? Start with **Starter holds**.
2. Press Start. While the circle fills, squeeze and lift. While it empties, let go.
3. Breathe out as you lift. Breathe in as you relax.

Turn on Sound if you want a soft beep each time the phase changes. The timer runs in your browser, and nothing is saved.

## The routines

<Remember title="Dr. Dana, on Instagram">

Start with 3 seconds on and 3 seconds off. Build to 5, then 10 seconds, once the shorter hold feels easy. Then add quick flicks: squeeze as hard as you can for 1 second, rest for 1 second. Do 10 holds and 10 flicks, twice a day, while you brush your teeth.<Ref n={2} />

</Remember>

The NICE guideline asks for at least 8 squeezes, 3 times a day, kept up for at least 3 months.<Ref n={1} /> The 4 routines in the timer fit inside that.

## Get the squeeze right

- **Lift** as if you hold in gas. Picture stopping your stream, but practice off the toilet.
- **Keep your butt and inner thighs relaxed.** Only the pelvic floor works.
- **Breathe through each squeeze.** Holding your breath pushes down.
- **Relax fully between reps.** A muscle has to lengthen before it can lift with full strength.

## How long until it works

In trials, women who trained for 3 to 6 months reported fewer leaks than women who did not.<Ref n={3} /> The timer helps with the hard part: doing it every day.
