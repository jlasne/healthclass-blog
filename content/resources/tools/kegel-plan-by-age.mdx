## The dose is the same at every age

The NICE guideline gives one dose for every woman: at least 8 squeezes, 3 times a day, for at least 3 months.<Ref n={1} /> The plan above follows it.

What your age changes is the plan around it. Your starting hold sets the first 4 weeks. Your age sets the positions and one thing to focus on.

## Leaks rise with age

In a US survey of 1,961 women, moderate to severe leaks rose with each age group.<Ref n={2} />

- **20 to 39:** 6.9% of women.
- **40 to 59:** 17.2% of women.
- **60 to 79:** 23.3% of women.
- **80 or older:** 31.7% of women.

In a Cochrane review, women who trained their pelvic floor reported fewer leaks than women who did not.<Ref n={3} /> Starting at any age counts.

## How the plan builds

<Remember title="Dr. Dana, on Instagram">

Start with 3 seconds on and 3 seconds off. Build to 5, then 10 seconds, once the shorter hold feels easy. Then add quick flicks: 1 second on, 1 second off.<Ref n={4} />

</Remember>

- **Weeks 1 to 4:** the hold you can keep clean, lying down or sitting.
- **Weeks 5 to 8:** longer holds, sitting, plus quick flicks.
- **Weeks 9 to 12:** holds of up to 10 seconds, standing, or while you move.

Move up when your holds stay clean to the end. A shorter, full hold trains the muscle better than a long one that fades.

## Keep it going

After 12 weeks, keep a short routine on the days you pick. Muscle loss with age raises your risk of pelvic floor problems, and strength training protects you.<Ref n={4} />
