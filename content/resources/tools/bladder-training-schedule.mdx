## How bladder training works

You pee at set times instead of each time an urge calls. Each week, you stretch the gap a little. Your bladder learns to hold more, and urges come less often.

The NICE guideline offers bladder training for at least 6 weeks as the first step for urge or mixed leaks.<Ref n={1} /> In a 1991 trial of 123 women aged 55 and older, it cut leaks by 57% on average.<Ref n={2} />

## How to set your schedule

1. **Find your gap.** Keep a [3-day bladder diary](/resources/tools/bladder-diary) and look at the time between your daytime trips.
2. **Start there.** Pee when you get up, then at your set times.
3. **Add 15 minutes a week,** once this week's gap feels doable.<Ref n={3} />
4. **Aim for 3 to 4 hours** between trips.
5. **At night, go when you wake up.** The schedule is for the day.

## When an urge comes early

Stop and stay still. Do 5 quick squeezes, breathe slowly, then distract yourself. The [urge wave timer](/resources/tools/urge-wave-timer) walks you through it in about 80 seconds.
