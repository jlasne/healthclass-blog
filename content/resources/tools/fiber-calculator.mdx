## Why fiber matters for your pelvic floor

Each time you strain on the toilet, you push down hard on your pelvic floor. Over years, those pushes add up.

In a 1994 study, 61% of women with prolapse had strained at stool as young adults. So had 30% of women with stress leaks, against 4% of women with neither.<Ref n={2} />

Fiber keeps your stool soft and easy to pass, so you strain less.

## Where most women stand

More than 90% of US women eat less fiber than the guidelines ask. On average, the gap is about half the goal.<Ref n={3} />

## How to add fiber

<Remember title="From Dr. Dana's fiber guide">

Eat both kinds: soluble fiber softens, insoluble fiber bulks. Drink water as you add fiber: water is the activator. Go slow, a few grams a week. Food first: supplements alone are not enough.<Ref n={1} />

</Remember>

1. **Start where you are.** Use the calculator above for 1 day.
2. **Add 2 to 3 g a day each week.** Half a cup of raspberries adds 8 g. A tablespoon of flaxseeds adds 2.8 g.
3. **Drink water with it.** Sip through the day, and aim for pale yellow urine.
4. **Check in after 2 weeks.** Softer stool and less straining tell you it works.
