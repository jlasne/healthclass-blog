## How they are alike

Elvie Trainer and Perifit work the same way. You insert a small sensor, open the app, and see each squeeze on your phone, live.<Ref n={2} /><Ref n={4} /> Both apps work only with their own device.

Both help you train every day. That habit is the first step guidelines list for stress leaks, for at least 3 months.<Ref n={6} />

## How they differ

**Price and stock.** Perifit Care lists at $149 and Care+ at $199, and both were on sale on November 9, 2026.<Ref n={3} /> Elvie Trainer lists at $199 and showed as sold out that day.<Ref n={1} />

**Returns.** Perifit offers a free return within 100 days.<Ref n={3} /> Elvie Trainer cannot go back once its seal is broken, unless it is faulty.<Ref n={1} />

**The app.** Elvie's app is free, with no in-app purchases.<Ref n={2} /> Perifit's app is free too, with an optional Premium plan for extra games, yoga and articles.<Ref n={5} />

## The option with no device

Some women prefer not to insert anything. Health Class is a 6-week class by a urologist: you follow the cues on your phone and train with your own muscles. It costs {app.price}.
