## What both get right

Both help you train your pelvic floor a little, every day. That habit is the first step guidelines list for stress leaks, for at least 3 months.<Ref n={5} />

## Where they differ

**Perifit is a sensor.** You insert a small device, and the app shows each squeeze live. Your squeeze moves a game on the screen.<Ref n={2} /> The app works only with the device.

**Health Class is a class.** Dr. Dana Kivlin, a board-certified urologist, teaches it. Each week opens with a short lesson. Each day has one guided exercise, with cues like "Breathe out, then lift." There is nothing to insert.

## A word on price

Perifit starts with the device: $149 for Perifit Care or $199 for Care+, at regular prices on its store.<Ref n={1} /> The app is free, with an optional Premium plan.<Ref n={3} /> Health Class costs {app.price}.

## Can you use both?

Yes. Some women like a sensor to check their squeeze and a class to learn what to do with it. Ask your doctor or a pelvic floor physio what fits you, above all during pregnancy or after birth.
