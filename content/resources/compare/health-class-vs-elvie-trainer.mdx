## What both get right

Both help you train your pelvic floor a little, every day. That habit is the first step guidelines list for stress leaks, for at least 3 months.<Ref n={4} />

## Where they differ

**Elvie Trainer is a sensor.** You insert a small device. As you squeeze, a gem rises on your phone, and the app flags a wrong squeeze.<Ref n={2} /> The app works only with the device.

**Health Class is a class.** Dr. Dana Kivlin, a board-certified urologist, teaches it. Each week opens with a short lesson. Each day has one guided exercise, with cues like "Breathe out, then lift." There is nothing to insert.

## A word on price and stock

Elvie lists the Elvie Trainer at $199, and its app is free.<Ref n={1} /><Ref n={3} /> On November 8, 2026, the store showed it as sold out. Health Class costs {app.price}.

If you want a sensor, compare 2 or 3 before you buy: price, return policy and stock differ. Our [Health Class vs Perifit](/resources/compare/health-class-vs-perifit) page covers Perifit, which has a 100-day free return.
