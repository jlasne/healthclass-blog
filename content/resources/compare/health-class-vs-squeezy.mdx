## What both apps get right

Both apps help you do the same thing: train your pelvic floor a little, every day. That habit is the first step guidelines list for stress leaks, for at least 3 months.<Ref n={4} />

Both remind you to train. Both show your progress. Both teach you what the pelvic floor does.

## Where they differ

**Squeezy is a plan.** Physiotherapists working in the NHS designed it.<Ref n={3} /> You get a pre-set exercise plan, prompts and reminders.<Ref n={1} /> A physio can tailor the plan in its professional mode.

**Health Class is a class.** Dr. Dana Kivlin, a board-certified urologist, teaches it. Each week opens with a short lesson. Each day has one guided exercise, with her cues on the screen.

## A word on price

Squeezy costs $4.99 once in the US App Store.<Ref n={1} /> Health Class: {app.price}. If one low price matters most, Squeezy wins. If you want to try a guided class first, start with our week 1.
