## You are far from alone

In a US survey from 2015 to 2018, 61.8% of women said they had leaked in the past 12 months.<Ref n={2} /> A sneeze is one of the moments that can bring it on.

## Why a sneeze pushes urine out

**Pressure from above.** A sneeze squeezes your belly hard and fast. That pressure lands on your bladder.

**Support from below.** Your pelvic floor is a hammock of muscle under your bladder. When it lifts in time, it holds the exit closed.

**Timing.** A sneeze takes a split second. If the hammock lifts late or softly, a few drops get out.

## What helps

1. **The Knack.** Squeeze and lift just before the sneeze. Hold through it. Then let go. In a small study, a squeeze before a medium cough cut leakage by 98%.<Ref n={1} />
2. **Daily training.** Slow holds build strength. Quick flicks build speed. Guidelines list pelvic floor training for at least 3 months as the first step for stress leaks.<Ref n={3} />
3. **A head start on allergy season.** Practice the Knack on fake coughs for 1 to 2 weeks before your season starts. Then every real sneeze becomes a rep.

## Try it today

Sit tall. Breathe in. As you breathe out, squeeze and lift as if you hold in gas and pee. Give a small fake cough while you hold. Then let go fully. Do it 5 times.
