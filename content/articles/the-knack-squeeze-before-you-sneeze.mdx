Stress leaks have triggers you can feel coming. A sneeze. A cough. A laugh. A heavy box.

That gives you about 1 second of warning. The Knack puts that second to work.

**The Knack: squeeze and lift your pelvic floor just before the pressure hits. Hold through the sneeze. Then let go.**

Here is the fine print. This was 1 small study of selected women. A larger randomized trial was designed in 2021 to test the Knack on its own.<Ref n={2} />

I teach it anyway. It costs nothing, takes 1 second, and builds a reflex you keep for life.

## Learn it in 1 week

- **Days 1 and 2: fake coughs.** Squeeze, cough, let go. 5 times, twice a day.
- **Days 3 and 4: standing up.** Squeeze first, then rise from your chair.
- **Days 5 and 6: lifting.** Squeeze before you pick up a bag or a child.
- **Day 7 onward: real life.** Every sneeze and every laugh is a rep.

<Try title="5 Knack coughs">

Stand tall. Breathe in. Squeeze and lift your pelvic floor. Keep the lift and cough once. Let go fully. Rest 5 seconds.

Repeat 5 times.

</Try>

## Make it automatic

A reflex needs reps tied to moments you already have. Pair the Knack with 3 cues:

1. Before you stand up.
2. Before you lift anything heavier than a bag of groceries.
3. The moment you feel a sneeze tickle.

The Knack works best on a pelvic floor that can lift fast. That is why it comes after the basics: in the app, it is week 4 of Pelvic Floor 101 and week 3 of Bladder leaks.
